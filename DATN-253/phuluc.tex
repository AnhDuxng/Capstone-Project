\chapter{Project Execution Timeline and Engineering Schedule}
\label{app:timeline}

The research, engineering, and empirical evaluation of this Capstone Project were conducted over a structured \textbf{15-week development lifecycle}. To ensure methodical execution, rigorous verification, and clear traceability of deliverables, the project was organized into four sequential engineering stages:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1: Research, Requirements Formalization, and Architecture (Weeks 1--3)}: In-depth analysis of Phase 2 operational bottlenecks (specifically SD-JWT library complexity, non-standard secp256k1 curves, and recurring IPFS pinning fees), formalization of the system specification in \texttt{SPEC.md} (56~KB), database schema modeling via Prisma ORM v6, and generation of 10,000 synthetic graduation records for empirical stress testing.
    \item \textbf{Stage 2: Smart Contracts, Cryptographic Engine, and Bulk Issuing (Weeks 4--7)}: Implementation of \texttt{IssuerRegistry.sol} and \texttt{CredentialRegistryV3.sol} featuring \texttt{Ownable2Step} and bitmap revocation; construction of the modular 6-package TypeScript SDK; achieving 100\% smart contract test coverage (52 test cases); and developing the chunked bulk issuance engine (\texttt{bulk.ts}, $20 \times 500 = 10,000$ credentials).
    \item \textbf{Stage 3: Full-Stack Web Portals and Asynchronous Job Manager (Weeks 8--11)}: Engineering four specialized Next.js 14 web portals (Issuer, Holder, Verifier, Admin); integrating Privy passwordless email authentication for graduates; building the asynchronous \texttt{BulkJobManager} with Server-Sent Events (SSE) live progress streaming; and implementing the interactive 10-step visual verification pipeline.
    \item \textbf{Stage 4: Empirical Benchmarks, Security Hardening, and Thesis Defense (Weeks 12--15)}: Executing the benchmark suite across Scenarios A through E (809 signatures/sec, 27.54s 10k bulk issuance, 5.502 ms verification latency, gas profiling); anchoring a live 10,000-credential batch on Ethereum Sepolia Testnet; conducting STRIDE threat analysis, Slither static audit, and passing 33/33 exploit simulations; and finalizing the graduation thesis manuscript, demonstration media, and presentation slide deck.
\end{enumerate}
